# 💡 My Understanding of DevOps

For me, DevOps is a culture that connects the Development and Operations teams to build, test, deploy, and manage applications efficiently.

It focuses on collaboration, automation, continuous integration, continuous delivery, and faster software deployment while maintaining reliability and scalability.

I believe DevOps is not just about tools—it's about improving the way teams work together.
